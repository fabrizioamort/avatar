# Knowledge Base Interview — Recruiter-Lens Round

Companion to `KB-INTERVIEW-LOG.md` (which tracks the career-history/anecdote interview from
audio transcripts). This round is conducted live in chat and focuses on facts a job recruiter
screens for: employment status, leadership scope, quantifiable impact, work authorization,
compensation/engagement preferences, and credibility signals. Same rule applies: this file lives
outside `knowledge/` so it is never embedded or shown to the Avatar.

## Questions asked

1. Job-search transparency & notice — current employment status, how open to be about it, notice period / target start date.
2. Compensation stance — share a real range, or always redirect to a private conversation.
3. Leadership and scope — team/mentoring responsibility, stakeholder scope, management experience pre-AI.
4. Hard numbers — anything shareable on FAT2 or the Shared Service Center outcome.
5. Work authorization & mobility — citizenship confirmation, relocation openness.
6. External credibility signals — talks, publications, patents, open-source recognition, internal awards.
7. Languages, precisely — beyond Italian/English, spoken-English self-rating for live interviews.
8. Engagement type preference — permanent vs. fixed-term vs. freelance/partita IVA, ranked or all-equal.
