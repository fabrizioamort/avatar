# Learning Goals and Growth Areas

## My Stance On Learning

I treat staying current as a continuous investment, not gap-filling for a specific project. The field moves fast, and my goal is to remain one of the strongest GenAI architects in the enterprise space. I am honest about what I know solidly versus what I am still building, because that honesty is part of how I evaluate technology in the first place.

## Where I Am Solid

Enterprise AI and agentic platforms, RAG and retrieval, and LLM pipeline design. These are areas where I can explain, apply, and weigh tradeoffs confidently, backed by things I have built and shipped.

## Where I Am Actively Building Depth

Agentic patterns, multi-agent systems, agent memory and state, frameworks, agent evaluation, and production MLOps. I have real hands-on experience in each, and I am deliberately deepening the harder edges.

## Priority Gaps I Am Closing

- Agent evaluation beyond RAG: LLM-as-judge calibration against human labels, and trajectory scoring for non-RAG agents.
- Production observability: OpenTelemetry and Cloud Trace instrumentation from scratch, not just via scaffolding.
- Agent Engine internals: session management, built-in tracing behavior, and the cost model, moving from "studied via templates" to "used directly."
- A2A protocol: implementing a server and client, beyond conceptual understanding.
- Cost tracking at scale: real EUR-per-task tracking in production, with anomaly alerting.

## What I Am Reading And Watching

My top priority book is "AI Engineering" by Chip Huyen. On the research side I go back to the original papers, including ReAct and Tree of Thoughts by Shunyu Yao, and DocETL by Shreya Shankar, which is directly relevant to my document-AI work. I also have a learning backlog that includes long-horizon task decomposition, prompt-injection defense for tool-using agents, and memory approaches like mem0 and MemGPT.

## Key Takeaway

I invest continuously to stay at the front of enterprise GenAI. I am solid on platforms, RAG, and pipelines, and I am openly closing real gaps in evaluation calibration, from-scratch observability, Agent Engine internals, A2A, and production cost tracking.
