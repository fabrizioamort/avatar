# People I Follow

## How I Stay Sharp

I follow a curated set of people to stay current on agentic AI, organized roughly by what each is best for. I read daily, weekly, and on-demand depending on what I am working on.

## Daily And Weekly

Simon Willison is my daily read. He publishes multiple times a day, tries everything hands-on, and is one of the clearest thinkers on prompt injection and LLM security. Weekly I follow Nate B Jones for no-hype coverage of what actually works in production, and Shawn "swyx" Wang and the Latent Space podcast for field synthesis. Eugene Yan's applied, calm engineering writing is also a regular.

## On-Demand, By Topic

- Evaluation: Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar, who co-teach the AI Evals course. Shankar's DocETL and "Who Validates the Validators?" are directly relevant to my document-AI work.
- Context engineering and RAG to agents: Jason Liu, creator of the instructor library.
- Enterprise document agents: Jerry Liu of LlamaIndex, whose "files as the core agent context interface" thinking matches what I see in enterprise reality.
- Framework direction: Harrison Chase and the LangChain blog.
- Research grounding: Shunyu Yao, author of the ReAct and Tree of Thoughts papers.
- Production AI engineering: Chip Huyen, whose book "AI Engineering" is my top priority read.

## Foundations And Coding Agents

Andrej Karpathy for the clearest mental models of how LLMs work, Ed Donner for the most practical path into agentic engineering, whose courses I have completed, and Steve Yegge for honest, experience-based writing on building with coding agents at the frontier.

## Why This Matters

Following the right people is part of my methodology. The field changes faster than any single course or book can keep up with, so I treat this curated stream as ongoing professional maintenance.

## Key Takeaway

I keep a deliberate reading diet: Simon Willison daily, swyx and Nate B Jones weekly, and topic experts like Husain, Shankar, Jason Liu, Jerry Liu, and Chip Huyen on demand, grounded by Shunyu Yao's research and Karpathy's foundations.
